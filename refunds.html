Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Revised: May 15, 2026

At Estate Spark (estatespark.co.uk), we strive to provide high-fidelity digital assets and professional service mandates. Our refund policy is designed to be fair to our international clients while protecting the intellectual property and time-intensive labor involved in our digital offerings.

MERCHANT OF RECORD NOTICE: All refund requests are ultimately processed via Paddle.com, our authorized reseller. Billing will appear as PADDLE.NET * ESTATE SPARK on your statement.

1. Digital Products (Instant Downloads)

In accordance with the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations regarding digital content:

  • No Refund Policy: Due to the nature of digital products (e.g., Resume Alpha Kits, B2B Scripts, PDF Guides), these are considered "used" once the download or access link has been generated. By clicking "Purchase," you provide your express consent to waive the 14-day cooling-off period.
  • Exception: If you have purchased a digital product but have not yet clicked the download link or accessed the content, a refund may be requested within 7 days of purchase.

2. Professional Service Mandates

For custom services such as Technical Web Audits, SEO Support, and GitHub Deployments:

  • Cancellation: You may cancel a service mandate within 24 hours of purchase for a 100% refund, provided work has not yet commenced.
  • Partial Refunds: Once a project has been initiated (e.g., server access granted, audit started), we offer a pro-rata refund based on the stage of completion. A fixed administration fee of £50 applies to all service cancellations.
  • Completion: No refunds are issued once a Technical Audit or Web Support block has been completed and the final deliverables (video/code/PDF) have been sent.

3. Subscription/Retainer Services

For monthly Digital Operations Support:

  • You may cancel your monthly retainer at any time via your Paddle dashboard or by emailing awais@estatespark.co.uk.
  • Cancellations will take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. We do not provide refunds for partial months of service.

4. Technical Issues

If you experience a technical error with a digital download (e.g., a broken link or corrupted file), we will provide a replacement link or file within 24 hours of being notified. Technical failure on our part is the only grounds for a full refund on digital products.

5. Refund Request Process

To initiate a refund request, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact awais@estatespark.co.uk with the subject line: "Refund Request - [Order ID]".
  2. Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the request.
  3. Our team will review the request against our delivery logs and respond within 48 hours.
  4. Approved refunds will be processed via Paddle.com to the original payment method.
