5. Anti-Circumvention & Fee Protection

The Client acknowledges that the Consultant has invested significant time and resources in identifying, underwriting, and preparing the Asset for acquisition. The Client agrees that:

  • Non-Circumvention: The Client shall not, for a period of 12 months from the date of this Agreement, attempt to bypass the Consultant by engaging directly with the seller or the listing agent to acquire the Asset (or any other asset introduced by the Consultant) without the Consultant’s written consent.

  • Fee Entitlement: Should the Client, or any associate or entity acting on behalf of the Client, acquire the Asset within 12 months of its introduction by the Consultant, the full Acquisition Success Fee of £1,500 shall become immediately due and payable to the Consultant, regardless of whether the Consultant was involved in the final stages of the transaction.

  • Evidence of Breach: Any direct communication between the Client and the seller or agent regarding the Asset, without the Consultant’s prior involvement, shall be deemed a breach of this Agreement and shall trigger the immediate liability of the Success Fee.
