The Story

I bought this property at auction. It needed a full back-to-brick refurbishment. Absolutely everything needed doing, including structural works at the front of the property and all new floors throughout. Upstairs, all the joists had rot so we had to replace every single floor. I carried out the refurb myself over the weekends. At the time I was able to do it and didn't have any other commitments, so I thought to save cost I'd do all the work myself. This did cost me time as the refurb took ten months, but a normal refurb of this scale could be done in four to five months pretty easily.

How I Bought It

Sourced through auction. The property was in a poor state which put off most buyers, allowing me to secure it at a great price. Purchase price was £87,000 with no stamp duty to pay. Auction fees and solicitor fees came to £3,000. Building control fee was £750. We didn't need an architect as we were simply replacing like for like, and we didn't do any searches. Total costs came to £142,500 and we secured a mortgage for £150,000, pulling all our money back out plus £7,500 profit on day one.

The Refurbishment

Full back to brick refurb including structural works to the front elevation, all new floor joists upstairs due to rot, new floors throughout, new kitchen, new bathroom, full rewire, new heating system, and complete redecoration inside and out. Completion timeframe: 10 months.

Project Management

I managed the property and the entire refurb myself. I found all the materials and got everything to site. I also had some help from lads that work for me, so I paid them for their time and organised them going there whilst other jobs were going on.

Tenanting

I found a fantastic boutique letting agent who specialises in the Crewe and Stoke area. They manage a growing portfolio of over sixty properties and own twenty seven themselves, so they really understand the local market. I got on well with the director straight away and they came in, assessed the property, and advised on the best rental price. The property was tenanted almost immediately after the first viewing with a great long term tenant whose family lives on the same road, so she is likely to stay for fifteen to twenty years.
