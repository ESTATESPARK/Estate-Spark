Privacy Policy

Last Revised: May 15, 2026

At Estate Spark (estatespark.co.uk), we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our international clients and website visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Data We Collect

To fulfill your digital product orders and service mandates, we collect minimal personal data, including:

  • Identity Data: Full name and professional title.
  • Contact Data: Email address and billing address.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and operating system (collected via Paddle.js for fraud prevention).
  • Transaction Data: Details about payments to and from you and details of products/services purchased.

2. How We Collect Your Data

We use different methods to collect data from and about you:

Direct Interaction: You provide your Identity and Contact Data when purchasing an asset or requesting a technical audit.

Third-Party Reseller (Paddle): Our order process is managed by Paddle.com. They provide us with the necessary transaction data to fulfill your order. Estate Spark does not store or see your credit card or full financial details.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data based on the legal basis of Contractual Necessity and Legitimate Interests:

  • To register you as a new client.
  • To deliver digital products (Kits/Scripts) instantly via email.
  • To manage our professional relationship (notifying you of audit completions).
  • To prevent fraud and ensure secure transactions via our Merchant of Record, Paddle.

4. Data Retention

We will only retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Typically, transaction-related data is kept for 7 years to comply with tax regulations.

5. International Data Transfers

Estate Spark is operated from Peshawar, Pakistan. By using our services, you acknowledge that your data will be transferred and processed outside the UK/EEA. We ensure a similar degree of protection is afforded to it by ensuring at least one of the following safeguards is implemented:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
  • Use of specialized payment resellers (Paddle.com) that adhere to high global security standards.

6. Your Legal Rights (UK GDPR)

Under certain circumstances, you have rights under data protection laws in relation to your personal data, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction or erasure of your personal data.
  • Object to the processing of your data.
  • Request a transfer of your data (Data Portability).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection lead at awais@estatespark.co.uk.

7. Security Measures

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. All payment communications are encrypted via SSL/TLS protocols via Paddle’s infrastructure.

8. Changes to this Policy

Any changes we may make to our privacy policy in the future will be posted on this page and, where appropriate, notified to you by email.
